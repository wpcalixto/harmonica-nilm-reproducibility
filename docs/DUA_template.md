7. cite the Zenodo record and the associated publication in any publication, report, presentation, dataset, software package, or other research output resulting from the use of the restored data, and state that access to the original measurement values was granted under a Data Use Agreement;

8. promptly inform the Data Controller of any actual or suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or distribution involving the inverse-transformation key or the restored data and cooperate in reasonable measures intended to contain the incident.
